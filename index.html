About NeuroSpark

NeuroSpark is an interactive cognition awareness project developed to help visitors experience basic cognitive processes through simple activities.

AttentionChoosing what to focus on and ignoring the rest.
MemoryEncoding, storing and retrieving information; working memory holds a few items for a short time.
Inhibitory ControlHolding back an automatic response (like reading a word) to do what the task requires.
Reaction / ResponseHow quickly and accurately we respond to what we perceive.
Time PerceptionOur internal sense of how much time has passed.
Cognitive InterferenceWhen two pieces of information conflict, such as a word's meaning versus its ink colour.

NeuroSpark activities are educational demonstrations of cognitive processes and are not diagnostic psychological tests.
